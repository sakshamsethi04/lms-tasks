i would use BLE (bluetooth low energy) for wireless sensor
diagnostics because ble has low communication overhead and 
esp32 needs to transmit  small data like ir sensor readings 
fault status etc to nearby phone 

Latency: ble supports frequent transmission of small data 
packets and the readings and status reach nearby device with
less delay

in power consumption too , ble consumes less power than wifi 

The ESP32 supports BLE and can communicate with a phone using GATT
(Generic Attribute Profile). A GATT service can group diagnostic
information, while its characteristics can store individual
sensor readings and fault status
