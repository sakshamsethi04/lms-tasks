THE ROLE OF P IN PID: 

calculates the correction usually named as P by multiplying
the error (e) with a constant (Kp) which decides the intensity  
with which the bot reacts to the error , P=Kp*e

THE ROLE OF I IN PID:

I is the integral part of PID , it basically calculates the 
accumulated error over time usually by unequal motor speeds 
it helps eleminate it by calculating it and so we can use it 
to calculate the total change is speed and direction we need

THE ROLE OF D IN PID:

D(derivative) part of PID helps avoid the overshooting by 
corresponding to the rate of change of error.. 

so basically the sum of P, I and D calculates the total 
correction needed in the left and right wheel to help 
center the bot with the line . it'll basically allign it 
with the line 

i would choose PID in this obviously because realistically 
all these errors do come , and to counter these PID is good

proper tuning of kp and kd is also necessary as if not perfect
it might cause zigzagging 


LOST LINE HANDLING:

the robot detects line loss when all 5 ir sensors indicate 
that the black line is absent , so instead of continuing with the
normal pid algo, it enters a recovery mode ..

The robot stores the last known line position or sensor error and
searches in that direction at a reduced speed. Once the sensors 
detect the line again, normal PID control resumes.

THE PID output is then used to adjust motor speeds .. if the line 
is detected towards right then the left motor speed is increased
and the right motor speed is decreased and the opposite if 
the line is detected left ..
