in exploratory traversal the robot does not know the 
complete layout, it explores different parts , detectes
junctions and deadends , backtracks when necessary to build
a map

in optimal route replay, the robot determines a suitable route
from start to end once the maze is mapped .. and then follows 
the sequence of movements without exploring every branch again

replay is simpler because during replay , the map is already 
known so it only follows the planned movements which reduces
the time consumed
